## Description
Contract Whist Score Tracker keeps your game organized from first deal to final round.

What you can do:
- Start full or half games with your own start/end card settings
- Follow dealer and bid order automatically each hand
- Enter bids and tricks with validation to prevent common scoring mistakes
- Select trumps quickly, including no-trump rounds
- Add special rounds: Blind, No Trumps, and Miz
- Enforce zero-bid streak rules
- Review live leaderboard and detailed round breakdowns
- Keep recent games on-device for quick reference

Ideal for social tables and competitive groups who want consistent scoring and less admin.
